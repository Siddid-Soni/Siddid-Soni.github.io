## My role
Sole developer, freelance (2026). I built the Next.js application, with API routes for authentication, cart and checkout, the product catalogue and order management. I also owned delivery end to end: the VPS, Docker, GitHub Actions CI/CD, Cloudflare DNS and TLS, and Zoho transactional mail.

## The site
The storefront for Monsoon Coffee Co., a small-batch roaster sourcing coffees from farms in India. Visitors browse the current stash of single-origin lots, open a lot for its process and tasting notes, and shop with an account and a cart. Prices are shown in rupees for visitors in India.

## What's on it
- **Current stash:** each lot gets its own card with origin, process and price.
- **The roastery:** pages on sourcing, the lab's cupping process and the roaster itself.
- **Accounts and cart:** login, registration and a persistent cart.
- **Newsletter:** sign-up for new drops.
